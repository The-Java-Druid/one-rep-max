# Privacy Policy for 1RM Calculator

**Last updated:** October 30, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how your information is handled when you use the **1RM Calculator** mobile application (the "Application").

## 1. Overview
The Application is built as a simple, offline utility tool designed to calculate one-repetition maximums based on user input. We prioritize user privacy, which is why the Application does not require registration, does not track you, and does not collect any personal data.

## 2. Information Collection and Use
* **Personal Data:** The Application does **not** collect, store, transmit, or share any personally identifiable information (such as your name, email address, IP address, or location).
* **Usage Data:** All calculations (weight, repetitions, and calculated results) are processed locally on your device in real-time. This data is not stored permanently or transmitted to external servers.

## 3. Permissions
The Application operates with minimal permissions and does not require access to:
* Your device location
* Contacts or camera
* Network/Internet access (the app functions entirely offline)

## 4. Third-Party Services
The Application does not currently use third-party services (such as analytics frameworks, ad networks, or cloud databases) that collect information used to identify you.

## 5. Children's Privacy
Because the Application does not collect any personal data, it does not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under the age of 13.

## 6. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
